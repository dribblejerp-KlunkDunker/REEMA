🔒 Privacy — what this protects and what it does not
Protected: your message content is end-to-end encrypted (post-quantum hybrid) — the relay never sees plaintext.
Not yet protected: the relay still sees who talks to whom, when, and group membership; mixing and cover traffic only hide timing, and only when several messages share a window.
Transport: the client→relay link is TLS by default. Your IP is hidden from the relay only if you launched this page through the Tor shell (npm run messenger-tor to a remote relay); a direct loopback or remote connection still exposes your IP.
No messenger can protect: a compromised device, a group member who leaks, or correlation with the physical world. Anonymity is only as strong as the least careful member. See ANONYMITY.md for the full threat model.
